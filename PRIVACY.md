# Privacy Policy for Bado

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Welcome to **Bado** ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by **Mirko Vitale**. Your privacy is critically important to us. Bado is engineered with a **local-first** approach: we believe your car information, personal documents, and financial records belong solely to you.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Storage

### 1.1 Local-First Architecture
All vehicle data you enter into Bado (including vehicle make, model, license plate, mileage, maintenance history, fuel logs, and expiration dates) is stored directly on your iOS device using Apple SwiftData and secure local sandbox storage.

### 1.2 Documents & Photos
When you attach or scan vehicle registration cards, insurance certificates, or inspection receipts:
- The images and PDF files are stored strictly within the app's local sandbox container on your device.
- Document text recognition (OCR) is performed **100% on-device** using Apple's Vision framework. Your documents are never uploaded to any external server or proprietary cloud for text extraction.

### 1.3 Apple iCloud Sync
If you have Apple iCloud enabled on your device, Bado synchronizes your vehicle data using Apple's official CloudKit / iCloud services. This sync occurs directly between your personal device and your private Apple iCloud account, encrypted with Apple's security protocols. We have zero access to your iCloud data.

---

## 2. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Bado offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (**Bado PRO**). All financial transactions and payment processing are handled exclusively by Apple via the App Store (StoreKit). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card details or billing information.

---

## 3. Advertising & Analytics

The free tier of Bado displays advertisements using **Google AdMob**.
- In compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, Bado requests your permission before any personalized advertising tracking can occur.
- If you decline tracking, only non-personalized, contextual advertisements are served.
- Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and performance telemetry in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- Bado PRO subscribers experience a completely ad-free experience.

---

## 4. Location Services

Bado includes a "Nearby Workshops" feature that lets you find certified mechanics, tire shops, and inspection centers near your current location.
- Your location is accessed only when you actively use this feature.
- We do not track your location in the background or store your location history.

---

## 5. Third-Party Disclosures & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data or vehicle documentation to third parties.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Bado does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13.
