Summary: Bado is built on a Local-First architecture. Your vehicle data, expenses, and documents stay on your device or in your personal Apple iCloud account. We do not operate tracking servers or sell your personal data.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bado ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Mirko Vitale, handles your information when you use our iOS application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent and minimal data handling practices.

2. Data Collection and Processing

Bado is engineered to operate without requiring user registration or account creation on third-party backend servers. All application data is stored directly on your iPhone or iPad.

A. Vehicle Information

You can store information about your vehicles, including:

  • Vehicle specifications (make, model, year, license plate, mileage, engine details, fuel tank capacity).
  • Maintenance records, service invoices, parts replaced, and costs.
  • Refueling logs and fuel consumption statistics.
  • Custom reminders, notes, and maintenance checklists.

This information is strictly saved in your local device storage (UserDefaults and private application sandbox).

B. Document and Photo Scanning (On-Device OCR)

When you attach or scan vehicle documents (such as insurance certificates, vehicle registration, road tax receipts, or inspection reports):

  • All Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is performed 100% on-device using Apple's Vision framework (VNRecognizeTextRequest).
  • Your camera feed, photos, and PDF files are never uploaded to external AI servers or third-party cloud services for analysis.
  • Saved document files are kept within your device's sandboxed document directory.

C. Location Data

If you grant location permission:

  • Parking Location: The App records your vehicle's parking coordinates solely to assist you in remembering where you parked.
  • Nearby Services & EV Charging: The App uses your current location via Apple MapKit to search for nearby mechanic shops, body shops, tire specialists, and EV charging stations.
  • Location data is processed ephemerally on-device and is never transmitted to our servers or tracked in the background.

3. Cloud Sync and Backups (Apple iCloud)

If you have iCloud enabled on your iOS device, Bado can synchronize and back up your garage data using Apple's native iCloud Drive / CloudKit infrastructure:

  • All backups are stored directly in your private Apple iCloud container.
  • We have no access to your iCloud credentials, files, or backed-up garage data.
  • You can disable iCloud backup at any time from the in-app Settings menu or through your iOS device Settings.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

A. Google AdMob

For users on the free tier, the App may display advertisements served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads):

  • In compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, the App requests your explicit permission before accessing the advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads.
  • If you decline tracking, non-personalized contextual ads will be displayed instead.
  • Subscribers of Bado PRO enjoy a completely ad-free experience, and all ad requests are disabled.
  • You can review Google's privacy policy at: policies.google.com/privacy.

B. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

Subscription and in-app purchase transactions are processed securely through Apple's App Store via StoreKit 2. We never collect or have access to your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data resides on your device:

  • You have full control over your records. You can modify or delete individual vehicles, maintenance logs, and documents at any time.
  • Deleting the App from your device permanently erases all locally cached files and records.
  • To delete cloud backups, you can manage your iCloud storage from the iOS Settings app (Apple ID > iCloud > Storage).

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
