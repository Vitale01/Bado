## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How do I restore my Bado PRO subscription?
If you reinstalled Bado or switched to a new iPhone:
1. Open **Bado**.
2. Go to the upgrade screen or Settings.
3. Tap **"Ripristina Acquisti" (Restore Purchases)**.
4. Your PRO features will activate immediately without any charge.

### 2. How does document scanning work?
You can upload PDF files or take photos of your vehicle registration, insurance, or inspection receipts. Bado uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to read dates and vehicle info automatically.

### 3. How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple:
1. Open your iPhone **Settings**.
2. Tap your **Apple ID** at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Select **Bado** and tap **Cancel Subscription**.

### 4. Why am I not receiving expiration notifications?
Make sure notifications are allowed:
Go to **Settings > Notifications > Bado** and ensure **"Allow Notifications"** is turned on.
