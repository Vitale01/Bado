Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

🚗 How does Bado track vehicle deadlines?
Bado tracks road tax (Bollo), mandatory safety inspections (Revisione), insurance policies (Assicurazione), and custom service milestones. You can enter dates manually or upload a PDF or image of your certificate to extract deadlines automatically using on-device optical character recognition (OCR).
🔄 How do I restore my Bado PRO subscription?
If you reinstalled the app or switched to a new iPhone, open Bado, go to the Garage or PRO upgrade screen, and tap "Ripristina Acquisti" (Restore Purchases). As long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID, your subscription will be restored instantly at no additional charge.
🔒 Is my vehicle paperwork and personal data safe?
Yes! Bado is built on a local-first architecture. All vehicle details, photos, and scanned documents remain strictly on your iPhone. When iCloud is enabled, your data synchronizes through your personal, end-to-end encrypted Apple iCloud container. We do not operate external web servers to collect or sell your vehicle documents.
🔔 Why am I not receiving expiry notifications?
Ensure notifications are enabled for Bado in your device settings: open Settings > Notifications > Bado and ensure "Allow Notifications", Sounds, and Badges are turned on.
💳 How do I cancel or modify my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled directly by Apple. To cancel or change plans, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your Apple ID profile at the top, select Subscriptions, find Bado, and choose "Cancel Subscription". You will retain access until the end of your prepaid billing period.
🤖 How does Bado Copilot (AI Assistant) work?
Bado Copilot answers questions regarding vehicle maintenance schedules, dashboard warning lights, tire rotations, and scheduled deadlines. It is an automated assistant designed for informational convenience. Always consult a certified mechanic for safety-critical repairs.
